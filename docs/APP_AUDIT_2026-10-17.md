## 1. Summary

A week after the last audit, much of the parcel sheet's worst behaviour is gone: enrichment calls dropped from 20–40 s to about 4 s, signed-out "Save parcel" answers in 0.35 s (was ~10 s), the dossier PDF renders in 3 s, the tablet sheet no longer hides its parcel, and the language choice now persists. The first-run guide dialog is a correct modal with sensible focus, and `/map_table` degrades to a readable fallback.

What is left falls into five groups:

1. **The sheet is too tall for what it shows, and breaks at 1024×768.** Its scrolling area shrinks to **52 px** (of a 436 px sheet) because the sticky footer takes ~190 px. The scrollable content is 5,000–8,000 px long, with about 20 section headings that have no body.
2. **Overlapping chrome.** The attribute table slides under the parcel sheet, the basemap popover covers the sheet's stat strip, panels cover the map attribution, and on a phone the map action column still covers the sheet's close button.
3. **Headline numbers are not parcel facts.** The stat strip shows a municipality flood share, a municipality risk score and a zone-wide OMI range as if they described the parcel; a road parcel (`STRADA297`) gets a sale price in one path and "—" in another.
4. **Legacy and public pages are still broken or stale.** `/landing` shows `0 / 0 / 0 / 0` stats, took 26 s once and is written for the old Leaflet product; `/map-legacy` still logs 404s and a Leaflet error; `/auth/register` shows every validation error on an untouched form; unknown URLs return raw JSON.
5. **Internal vocabulary and English leak into the user interface**, in both languages: `veneto__particelle`, `omi-gross-rental-yield-v1`, `aecs4u-stats PostgreSQL sister.v_sister_property_by_cadastral_parcel.address`, "Residential apartments" in an Italian sheet.

One thing could not be judged cleanly: server latency was **intermittent**. The first pass hit a 26 s `/landing`, a 503 on the layer health check, a search that sat on "Searching…", and a parcel-reference search that failed after ~39 s with no message. Minutes later the same endpoints answered in 0.15 s. See M15.

## 3. Findings

### High

#### H1. At 1024×768 the parcel sheet's scroll area is 52 px tall
- With a parcel selected at 1024×768 the sheet is 436 px high. Header 95 + stat strip 108 + tab strip 43 + **content 52** + SISTER button 51 + action buttons 87.
- The content region is the only thing that scrolls and holds everything the user came for. It shows one heading ("Cadastre") and nothing else. See `09-parcel-laptop.png`.
- At 1440×900 the same region is 393 px of 758 (52 %), which is also tight.
- The stat strip grows by 20 px at this width because "2500–5000 €/m²" wraps to three lines.
- **Fix:** collapse the footer (SISTER, five actions) into a single row or overflow menu below ~800 px of height; let the stat strip scroll away with the content; set a minimum content height and let the sheet extend to the bottom of the viewport.

#### H2. Mobile: the map action column covers the sheet's close button and header actions *(open since 10-09 H1)*
- At 390×844 the 38 px action column (`Basemap`, `?`, `€`, locate, copy link, …) sits at x=337 over the sheet. It overlaps the **Close parcel details** button, which `elementFromPoint` resolves to `map-round-button btn btn-light`. It also hides part of the PDF/link buttons, the "Sheet" stat cell and the right end of the tab strip.
- The sheet's tab strip and its bottom action row both scroll sideways, each with a visible scrollbar: "Find adjacent parc…" is cut off, and "Energy" is cut off in the tab strip. See `09-parcel-mobile.png`.
- **Fix:** hide or move the map action column while the sheet is open on narrow screens, or inset the sheet by the column's width. Wrap the action row instead of scrolling it.

#### H3. `/landing` is stale and partly broken *(open since 10-02 H6, 10-09 H10)*
- All four stat cards read `0 Regions / 0 Provinces / 0 Municipalities / 0 Cadastral Files`, because the page calls `/api/v1/get-regions/`, which returns 404.
- The copy describes the old product: "Leaflet Map", "Folium Map Generation", "SQLite-backed cache", "SpatiaLite databases", "Session State: Current GeoDataFrame persists across requests", a "Tech Stack" list. Nothing mentions the MapLibre `/map`, the parcel sheet, the layers, or the dossier.
- It loaded in **26.4 s** during the route sweep, with a 503 from `/api/v1/map/layers/health`.
- axe: two `color-contrast` and two `link-in-text-block` failures (footer "Health" and "API Docs" links), a skipped heading level, and content outside any landmark (`.stats-bar`).
- The page has no `<meta name="description">`.
- **Fix:** either rewrite it around the current product with real numbers (or none), or redirect `/landing` to `/map`. Drop the stats calls if the endpoint is gone.

#### H4. Panels cover the map attribution *(open since 10-09 H8)*
- With the layers panel, the parcel sheet or the attribute table open on desktop, the "© Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors" line is clipped to "© Esr…" (`02-layers-panel.png`) or "…Libre" (`06-parcel-sheet-desktop.png`). The map-layers card sits over it on mobile as well.
- This is a licence-compliance risk (OpenStreetMap, Esri) as well as a design problem.
- **Fix:** keep attribution above all panels (raise its z-index, or offset it left of the open panel).

#### H5. `/map-legacy` still logs errors on load *(open since 10-02 H4, 10-09 H11)*
- Console on first load: three 404s on `/api/v1/get-regions/`, a `fitBounds failed: Error: Bounds are not valid` warning from `autoZoomToAllPolygons`, plus the Clerk development-keys warning.
- axe: **critical** `meta-viewport` (the Folium container embeds its own `<meta name="viewport">`, which can block pinch-zoom), and 12 `region` violations.
- The language `<select>` renders as a flag with no text.
- The page loads a Google basemap with 20 red star markers over Italy while the status pill says "No data loaded".
- **Fix:** if the page is kept, fix the `get-regions` call and guard `fitBounds`; remove the inner viewport meta. If it is not, retire it.

#### H6. Internal identifiers and infrastructure names appear in the sheet in both languages *(open since 10-02 M5, 10-09 M6)*
Visible without any "advanced" toggle:
- `Source: veneto__particelle`, `Dataset: veneto__particelle · Match: parcel_reference`
- `omi-gross-rental-yield-v1`, `omi-zone-percentile-midrank-v1` (next to the 11,52 % yield and the percentile)
- `aecs4u-stats PostgreSQL sister.v_sister_property_by_cadastral_parcel.address`
- `modelview.v_map_sale_points` as the PVP source
- `Resolution municipality plus parcel-centroid nearest grid point`, `Match: PostGIS ST_Covers`, `Match: distance from parcel centroid`
- Layer descriptions in the layers panel: "Source version shown in feature details · aecs4u-stats PostgreSQL/PostGIS"
- In the Data tab: "Agenzia delle Entrate INSPIRE cadastral extract · veneto__particelle", "NASA Black Marble VIIRS VNP46A4 · VNP46A4.002".
- **Fix:** keep a human source name and date for users ("Agenzia delle Entrate — INSPIRE, 2024") and move table, view and version identifiers into a "Technical details" disclosure or the dossier's source register.

### Medium

#### M1. The sheet is very long and ~20 sections are bare headings
- Scroll height of the sheet content is **5,186–8,118 px** depending on the tab.
- There are 30 sections. On a road parcel after 45 s, about 20 of them (`Terrain`, `Land cover`, `Building footprint coverage`, `Civic addresses (ANNCSU)`, `Coastal change`, `Agenzia Demanio concessions`, `Ground movement`, `Criticality bulletin`, `Active fires`, `Municipality`, `Income`, `Night lights`, `Census 2021`, `Safety`, `Demographic indicators`, `Quality of life`, `Points of interest`, …) have a body of 6–27 characters, i.e. the heading only, with no "not available" text and no content.
- In Italian on parcel 246 the same list is still headings only.
- The "Data coverage" table already says which of 21 blocks are available (2/21 on the road parcel, 8/21 on parcel 246).
- *Correction:* the bare headings were sections that had **not loaded yet**, not sections that stayed empty. Sections load lazily when scrolled near (`IntersectionObserver`, 320 px margin), and an unloaded section renders only its heading. Sections that load and have nothing are already collapsed with "Not available" in their header.
- **Fix:** show a placeholder in sections that have not loaded yet.

#### M2. Stat-strip chips present municipality and zone data as parcel facts
- `Flood / landslide: High · 7,5%` and `Risk context: 24,7/100` are the whole-municipality share and score (the body says so: "describe the whole municipality"). The chips do not.
- `Sale price (OMI): 2500–5000 €/m²` is the OMI zone range; the same parcel's body says the OMI valuation uses a zone snapshot with "one prevailing type per zone" and no reference semester.
- For `STRADA297` (a road) the click path showed `2500–5000 €/m²`, `High · 7,5%` and `24,7/100`, while a deep-link reload of the same parcel showed `—` in all three and the body said "OMI valuation: Not available".
- **Fix:** label the scope in the chip ("Municipality flood share", "OMI zone B2") or move them out of the parcel strip. Make click and reload agree.

#### M3. Road/street parcels are presented like buildable parcels
- `STRADA297` is a road. It is titled "Parcel STRADA297", carries a price chip, a PV cash-flow calculator and a "Purchase cadastral query" button. Nothing tells the user it is public road.
- **Fix:** detect `STRADA`/`ACQUE`-type particelle and say what they are, and hide valuation and energy blocks for them.

#### M4. The attribute table slides under the parcel sheet
- On 1440×900 with a parcel selected, "Attribute table" opens a 484 px-high panel anchored at the left that runs under the sheet; its right-hand controls and the data grid are hidden behind it. The first view is all large form controls (`Cadastral parcels`, `Filter…`, `Rows 100`, `Attribute`, `Contains`) with no data row visible. See `15-attribute-table.png`.
- The controls are noticeably larger (≈36 px, 16 px text) than the rest of the interface (≈28 px, 13 px).
- **Fix:** dock the table to the left of the sheet (or close the sheet), put the grid first and the filters in a compact toolbar.

#### M5. The basemap popover opens over the sheet header
- The popover (`12-basemap.png`) covers the parcel sheet's stat strip and its action buttons. It lives in the action column, so it will always collide with an open sheet.
- **Fix:** open it to the left of the sheet, or as a menu below the button with a max-width that respects the sheet.

#### M6. Sheet tabs use `aria-selected` on plain buttons *(open since 10-09 M4)*
- *Correction:* the first two claims below are wrong. My script read `aria-selected` **or** `aria-current` and reported whichever it found; the six section buttons use `aria-current="true"` inside a labelled `<nav aria-label="Parcel sections">` (a correct pattern for in-page navigation), and the `<aside>` already has `aria-labelledby="directParcelTitle"`. Only the hash ids remain a finding.
- ~~`Value / Property / … / Data` are `<button>`s with `aria-selected` but no `role="tab"`~~ (they use `aria-current`).
- ~~The `<aside id="directParcelPanel">` has no label~~ (it is labelled by its title).
- Tab targets update the URL hash with Italian ids (`#immobile`, `#territorio`, `#contesto`, `#energia`, `#dati`, `#sezione-identity`) even when the UI is English.
- **Fix:** use a real tablist, give the aside `aria-label` (e.g. the parcel title), and use English or neutral hash ids.

#### M7. Basemap is blurred at the zoom where parcels matter
- At z17 the Esri Light/Dark raster is upscaled; labels like "Verona" are visibly blurred (`05-parcels-z17.png`, `11-dark-parcel.png`). Parcel numbers (vector) are sharp beside them, which makes the contrast obvious.
- **Fix:** cap the raster at its native zoom with `maxNativeZoom`/`maxzoom` and overzoom cleanly, or switch basemap at z≥17.

#### M8. Number formatting is inconsistent *(open since 10-09 M7)*
- *Correction:* the cause is not "inconsistent separators" but a hard-coded `it-IT` locale in `parcel-panel-core.js`, `map-v2.js` and `parcel-enrichment.js`, so the English UI printed Italian numbers. The missing thousands separator in `2500–5000` is **correct for Italian**: CLDR does not group four-digit numbers in `it`.
- English UI: `388,3 m²`, `7,5%`, `24,7/100` use decimal commas; `58.471` (solar chip) uses a dot as thousands separator.
- Italian UI: the formats are right.
- The solar chip `58.471` has no unit or label in the header row.
- **Fix:** format every number through one locale-aware helper (`Intl.NumberFormat`) and add the unit to the solar chip.

#### M9. Italian is incomplete in the shell and the sheet
- Still English with `lang="it"`: "Skip to main content", "Toggle sidebar navigation", "Toggle between light and dark theme", "Enter fullscreen", "Zoom in", "Zoom out", "Reset bearing to north", "Map ready".
- In the sheet: "Residential apartments", "Industrial warehouses" (OMI types), "Risoluzione OMI zone polygon", "Risoluzione municipality plus parcel-centroid nearest grid point", "Corrispondenza: distance from parcel centroid", "Fonte: … current zone snapshot …".
- The OMI rows read "Zona B2 · Residential apartments · Stato di conservazione prevalente". *Correction:* the state is not missing: the API's `stato_conservazione: "P"` **means** "prevalente" (the snapshot holds one prevailing condition per zone), so only the English type label is wrong.
- "Accedi per usare la tua selezione" for "Sign in to use your shortlist" is ambiguous ("selezione").
- **Fix:** translate the map-control labels (MapLibre locale) and the OMI type and resolution strings; render the conservation state.

#### M10. `/auth/register` shows every validation error on an untouched form
- On load, with nothing typed: "First name is required", "Last name is required", "Please provide a valid email", "Password must be at least 8 characters", "Passwords do not match", "You must agree to the terms and conditions" (`23-register.png`).
- The logo is a white tile with a black icon on the dark theme.
- axe: `aria-progressbar-name` (`#passwordStrength`), three `color-contrast` failures (the helper text and the terms link), `image-redundant-alt` on the logo. `/auth/login` has the same redundant alt.
- **Fix:** show errors only after blur or submit; give the strength meter an accessible name; fix contrast.

#### M11. `/map_table` depends on a Bokeh server that rejects the connection
- Four console errors: `WebSocket … ws://127.0.0.1:5006/dashboard/ws failed … 403`, then three Bokeh messages.
- The page handles it well ("Interactive table unavailable … Open the map attribute table", `27-map-table.png`), but a 403 on the websocket looks like an origin allow-list problem, not an outage, and the page does not use the same shell as the rest of the app.
- **Fix:** add the app origin to Bokeh's `allow_websocket_origin`, or drop the route in favour of the built-in table.

#### M12. Unknown URLs return raw JSON
- `/this-does-not-exist` and `/cadastral-data` respond `{"detail":"Not Found"}` as a bare document with no title, `lang` or viewport meta (`24-404.png`). Every other page is styled.
- **Fix:** a styled 404 for requests that accept HTML, with a link back to the map.

#### M13. No search or app metadata anywhere *(open since 10-02 L1, 10-09 M11)*
- None of the 19 routes has a `<meta name="description">`; `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/help`, `/contact`, `/notifications` have titles but nothing else; I did not check Open Graph or canonical tags, but a shared parcel link has no description to preview.
- **Fix:** add description and Open Graph tags to the shells (a parcel link could carry the parcel title).

#### M14. Keyboard focus is lost after common actions
- After "Skip tour" on the guide dialog the focus is on "Skip to main content" (the top of the page); it should return to the control that opened the guide, or to the search box.
- After clicking "Save parcel" the focus is also at the start of the document, so a keyboard user starts from the top after every action in the sheet. *Cause (found while fixing):* the handler sets `disabled = true` on the button, which drops focus; `document.activeElement` was `<body>`, whose text begins with the skip link, which is why my script printed "Skip to main content". The same happened for "Find adjacent parcels".
- Escape does not close the Layers panel (`aria-expanded` stays `true`, focus stays on the toggle), although it closes the basemap popover, the guide and the purchases dialog.
- **Fix:** restore focus to the invoking element, and handle Escape in the layers panel.

#### M15. Failures are silent in search
- During the first pass a parcel-reference search (`/api/v1/map/search?query=L781 5 100`) returned 503 after ~39 s and the interface showed no message; an earlier "Veron" search sat on "Searching… 2s" with an empty list.
- The same queries answered in 0.15–0.3 s later (and returned `[]` for the parcel reference, which the UI then reports as "No result").
- **Fix:** a client timeout (~8 s) with "Search is slow, try again", and a visible error on 5xx that is different from "No result".

#### M16. Console noise from expected conditions
- `POST /api/v1/saved-parcels` → 401 when a signed-out visitor presses "Save parcel". *Correction:* I first wrote that this happens on every signed-out `/map` load; it came from my own click. Page load does not call it.
- `GET /api/v1/get-regions/` → 404, seven times across `/map-legacy`, `/landing`, `/data-sources`.
- `GET /api/v1/enrichment/municipality/L781` returned 404 once during a deep-link load; it returned 200 in 0.06 s on three later calls, so it is intermittent.
- Four Clerk development-keys warnings.
- These hide real errors in monitoring. **Fix:** do not call authenticated endpoints when signed out; remove the dead `get-regions` calls.

#### A1 (High). The first auction request after every restart waits minutes with no explanation
- The store loads the national snapshot (about 717,000 geocoded sales) in one query with a 300 s statement timeout. The route called `map_points(wait=True)` and simply awaited it, so the first visitor's request stayed open for 100–230 s. The client only retries on 503, which this path never returned, so the user saw "Loading auction listings… N s" and nothing else.
- On a loaded machine the load can exceed its own 300 s timeout, fail, and start over on the next request.
- Seen again during verification: after a restart the load query (`pvp_enriched`, `SELECT sale_id, latitude, longitude, …`) was still running at **264 s of its 300 s limit** with the machine at a load average of 9–60 (parallel test runs and other dev services). A load that exceeds the limit fails, and before this change every poll afterwards started another 300 s query.
- **Fix:** a bounded wait (`MAP_SALES_LOAD_WAIT_SECONDS`, default 12 s) then `503 Retry-After: 10` while the shielded load continues; the client now retries 24 times instead of 6. A failed load backs off for `MAP_SALES_FAILED_LOAD_BACKOFF_SECONDS` (60 s) instead of restarting on the next request, and the 300 s statement limit is configurable (`MAP_SALES_LOAD_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`).

#### A2 (High). Filtering the national snapshot runs on the event loop
- `PvpSalesStore.map_points` scanned up to 717k points in Python on the loop thread: ~1.5 s for an Italy-wide box and **18 s** with no `bbox`, during which no other request (pages, tiles, health) was served. This is a plausible cause of the "everything stalls" behaviour seen in the first pass (26 s `/landing`, search stuck on "Searching…").
- **Fix:** the scan moved to `asyncio.to_thread` (`_select_points`).
- **Not changed:** `GET /sales/map-points` with no `bbox` still returns the whole national feed (41.6 MB) because "existing consumers" depend on it; the JSON encoding of that response still happens on the loop. Callers should pass `bbox`.

#### A3 (High). `refresh=1` is public and unthrottled
- Any visitor can force a rebuild of the national snapshot; one call did not return in 116 s. Combined with A1 this lets one request keep the loader busy indefinitely.
- **Fix:** a forced refresh within `MAP_SALES_FORCED_REFRESH_MIN_SECONDS` (300 s) of the last successful load is answered from memory.

#### A4 (Medium). "Highlight auction sales" downloads whole parcel pages to keep a handful
- The client pages through `cadastral-parcels/features` (up to 5 pages, 25,000 candidates, several MB each) and discards everything that is not `is_auction_sale` in the browser.
- **Fix:** a database-side `flag=is_auction_sale` filter on `/map/layers/{layer}/features` (allow-listed column names only); the client sends it. Verified at Verona z15: the auction request is now **518 ms and 3 KB** for the same 14 parcels (was 3.8 s + 2.5 s and 2 × 533 KB).
- **Not changed:** the default-on "Highlight requests" (Visura) overlay uses the same download-and-discard pattern (3.8 s, 533 KB at z15). It counts parcels whose flag is unknown (`has_visura` is nullable), which a plain `IS TRUE` filter would lose, so it needs its own change.

#### A5 (Medium). National-scale listings are an arbitrary 0.7 % sample, and one cluster hides most of the data
- An Italy-wide request returns the 5,000 points nearest the box centre out of 717,104, which is not representative; the map correctly refuses below z10 but offers no overview.
- At Verona z13, one cluster holds **986 of 1,376 listings (72 %) at a single coordinate**: sales geocoded to the municipality centroid. The listings carry an `approximate` flag (drawn at lower opacity), but clustering merges exact and approximate points.
- **Not changed** (needs a product decision): server-side clustering for overviews, and keeping approximate points out of the clusters.

#### A6 (Low). The progress bar for "Drawing sale markers…" never finishes
- Data arrived in under a second and the clusters were visible, yet the bottom progress card stayed on "Loading auction listings… 20 s / Drawing sale markers…" for more than 20 s: the promise waits for a MapLibre `sourcedata` event with `isSourceLoaded` that does not always arrive for a clustered GeoJSON source.
- **Fix:** the wait ends after 3 s regardless. Verified: the card clears after 3.6 s (it stayed for more than 20 s before); the listings request itself takes 82 ms and 13 KB.

#### A7 (Low). `sales/geo-layers` returns 503 where it is not configured
- `GET /api/v1/sales/geo-layers` answers `503 {"error": "sales geographic statistics are not configured"}`. The page only requests it when configured, so no user impact, but a 501/404 would not read as an outage in monitoring. **Not changed.**

## 4. What worked

- **Performance of the map shell:** TTFB 26 ms, DOMContentLoaded 211 ms, load 232 ms on a warm cache; the HTML is 54 KB gzipped; 38 requests at load, 13 first-party.
- **Search when the server is healthy:** a combobox with live results in 0.9 s; `Verona` returns "Verona" and "Villafranca di Verona", each marked "Municipality"; a no-match query reports "No result".
- **Enrichment latency:** `parcel/by-reference` 15 ms–0.8 s, `omi/quotes` 72–100 ms, `buildings` 66 ms, `mps04/pga` 126 ms, `parcel/details` ~4 s. The dossier PDF is 386 KB in 3.1 s and a valid PDF 1.7 file; the deprecated report still answers in 0.5 s.
- **Signed-out behaviour:** "Save parcel" says "Sign in to save parcels" within 0.35 s; the shortlist button, purchases dialog and favourites star all lead to sign-in cleanly.
- **First-run guide:** three steps, a real `<dialog>` that is modal, focus on "Next", "Skip tour" available, readable at 390 px.
- **Dark theme:** the toggle changes the basemap with it (`basemap=dark` in the URL), and the sheet, popovers and dialogs are legible (`11-dark-parcel.png`). `/privacy` follows the OS scheme.
- **Keyboard:** the Tab walk on `/map` has 36 stops, each with a visible focus indicator, a working skip link, and a logical order apart from "Layers" coming after the action column.
- **Language persistence:** switching to Italiano sets `lang="it"`, translates most of the shell and the sheet, and survives a plain reload of `/map`.
- **URL state:** `lat`, `lng`, `zoom`, `layers`, `parcel` and `parcel_id` restore the view and selection. The selected parcel gets a clear outline and fill in light and dark.
- **Responsive layout:** no horizontal scroll on any page at 390, 768, 1024 or 1440; the sheet is 480–518 px at desktop and tablet and a full-width sheet at 390.
- **Operations and security:** `/health` and `/ready` return 200; HSTS, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy` and a restrictive `Permissions-Policy` are set; the axe scan of `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/help`, `/contact`, `/notifications` is clean.
- **Honest data labelling:** the sheet states that hazard figures are municipality-wide, that PGA is a nearest-grid-point estimate, that the OMI estimate is not a valuation, and that "missing data are never shown as zero".
